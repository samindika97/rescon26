Plenary Speaker

Prof. Ross Mitchell

Professor, State Key Laboratory of Lithospheric and Environmental Coevolution
Institute of Geology & Geophysics, Chinese Academy of Sciences
No. 19, Beitucheng West Road, Beijing, China
Email: ross.mitchell@mail.iggcas.ac.cn
Professor Ross Mitchell is a Professor at the Institute of Geology and Geophysics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IGG-CAS), where he has served since 2020. He previously held the position of Associate Professor at IGG-CAS (2019–2020), Research Fellow at the School of Earth and Planetary Sciences, Curtin University (2016–2019), and Postdoctoral Fellow in the Division of Geological and Planetary Sciences at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech) (2013–2016). He received his BA in Geology from Carleton College in 2007 and his Ph.D. in Geology and Geophysics from Yale University in 2013, under the supervision of Professor David A. D. Evans. His research focuses on how the Earth works, from the core to the crust, with particular expertise in paleomagnetism, cyclostratigraphy, and Earth history. His work seeks to understand the dynamics of past supercontinents and develop systems-based approaches to understanding and predicting future supercontinents. He served as Secretary of UNESCO IGCP 648 and has been a member of the FORES Leadership Group since 2021.
His distinctions include the National Science Foundation Antarctica Service Medal, Yale University's Philip M. Orville Prize and Estwing Hammer Prize, the Young Scientist Award at the 6th International Dyke Conference, and recognition through the CAS PIFI Special Expert and Young Talents Plan.
Title of the Talk
Science Writing: Tricks of th trade for high impact publishing
Plenary Speaker

Dr. S. Viswanath

Scientist G, Retd., ICFRE, MoEF&CC, Govt. of India
Former Director, KSCSTE-Kerala Forest Research Institute
Email: syam.viswanath@gmail.com
Dr. Syam Viswanath is a leading forestry scientist with more than 32 years of research and professional experience in forestry, agroforestry, and sustainable land-use practices. A native of Aluva, Kerala, he is currently based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. He obtained his Master’s degree in Forestry from Kerala Agricultural University and a Ph.D. from Kumaun University, Nainital, India. He also completed 10 months of specialized training in Agroforestry at the University of Florida, USA, under an FAO fellowship.
Dr. Viswanath served as the Director of the Kerala Forest Research Institute (KFRI) from 2018 to 2023, contributing significantly to forestry research, capacity building, and the promotion of sustainable agroforestry systems. He has published more than 140 research papers and has guided 25 Ph.D. students, demonstrating his substantial contribution to forestry research and education. His recognitions include the Dr. K. G. Tejwani Memorial Award (2021). In recognition of his contributions to forestry, a new fungal species discovered in Kerala was named Trichoglossum syamviswanathii in his honour.
Title of the Talk
Securing nutritional security, ecological and climate resilience in small farms: Food Forests shows how to do it.
Plenary Speaker

Prof. Weiqiang Wang

Senior Scientist, South China Sea Institute of Oceanology (SCSIO), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), Guangzhou, China
Director, China-Sri Lanka Belt and Road Joint Laboratory on Tropical Oceanography

Prof. Weiqiang Wang is a senior scientist at the South China Sea Institute of Oceanology (SCSIO), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), Guangzhou, China, with extensive international research experience in physical oceanography and marine sciences. His research focuses on large-scale ocean circulation and ocean–atmosphere interactions, contributing to a better understanding of ocean dynamics and their role in the Earth system.
Prof. Wang obtained his Bachelor’s (1995), Master’s (1998), and Ph.D. (2002) degrees in Physical Oceanography from Ocean University of Qingdao, China. He has held research and visiting positions at several leading international institutions, including Universität Hamburg, Germany (2007–2012); IFM-GEOMAR, Kiel, Germany (2006–2007); and the Atlantic Oceanographic and Meteorological Laboratory (AOML), NOAA, USA (2005–2006). He also conducted research at Lund University, Sweden, as a visiting scientist.
He has been associated with the South China Sea Institute of Oceanology since 1998 and has served as a senior scientist since 2012. His work integrates oceanographic observations and interdisciplinary approaches to advance understanding of ocean circulation and ocean–climate interactions. He is also the Director of the China-Sri Lanka Belt and Road Joint Laboratory on Tropical Oceanography.
